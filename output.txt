WEEK 11: RAG TEXT SUMMARIZATION
========================================
1. RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation, a modern AI framework.
2. It links a Large Language Model (LLM) with external data sources.
3. These sources can be private databases, APIs, or real-time information feeds.
4. By accessing these sources, the LLM can retrieve specific facts on demand.
5. This retrieval step supplements the model’s internal knowledge base.
6. Consequently, the system can answer questions with up-to-date information.
7. RAG reduces reliance on the model’s memorized training data alone.
8. It improves answer accuracy by grounding responses in verified sources.
9. The framework is useful for applications requiring current or proprietary data.
10. Overall, RAG enables more reliable and timely AI-generated outputs.
