Topic: a dragon and a knight
Story: Sir Gareth lowered his sword as the ancient dragon exhaled a gentle plume of warmth, rekindling the freezing knight's dying campfire. Decades of kingdom-mandated hatred dissolved in the glowing cavern light, leaving only two weary survivors of an endless war.

Topic: a lost spaceship
Story: For three centuries, the *Astraea* drifted through an uncharted void, her automated distress beacon pulsing toward an Earth that had long since forgotten her. Inside the frost-glazed bridge, the navigational computer flickered and died, unable to recognize a single constellation in the alien sky. Unanchored and blind, the silent ship carried its sleeping crew onward, no longer journeying toward a destination, but simply into forever.
